Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Do I need video editing experience? ↓
Zero. Paste a YouTube link, choose your duration, and the AI handles everything. No timeline editors, no technical skills required.
What YouTube videos work? ↓
Any public YouTube video. Best results on videos 10 minutes or longer — interviews, tutorials, vlogs, podcasts, motivational content.
Can I use this for my clients? ↓
Yes. The Agency plan gives you up to 10 seats with white-label options. Perfect for social media managers and content agencies.
Stripe vs Gumroad — what is the difference? ↓
Same access either way. Stripe accepts card, Apple Pay, Google Pay. Gumroad also accepts PayPal. Use whichever is easiest.
Can I cancel anytime? ↓
Yes. No long-term contracts. Cancel from your dashboard anytime and your access continues until the end of your billing period.
