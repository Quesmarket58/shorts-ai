Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Do I need video editing experience? ↓
Zero. Paste a YouTube link, choose your duration, and the AI handles everything. No timeline editors, no technical skills required.
What YouTube videos work with this? ↓
Any public YouTube video — interviews, tutorials, vlogs, podcasts, motivational content. Best results on videos 10 minutes or longer.
Can I use this for my clients? ↓
Yes — the Agency plan gives you up to 10 seats with white-label options. Perfect for social media managers and content agencies.

Same access either way. Stripe accepts card, Apple Pay, Google Pay. Gumroad also accepts PayPal. Use whichever is easiest for you.
How does the affiliate program work? ↓
You get a unique referral link. Every person who signs up for a paid plan through your link earns you $7.50/month recurring — as long as they stay subscribed.
Can I cancel anytime? ↓
Yes. No long-term contracts. Cancel from your dashboard anytime — your access continues until the end of your billing period.
